# Dyslexic Font.md

## Summary

Dyslexic fonts are specialized typefaces designed to enhance readability for individuals who experience dyslexia. These fonts use unique character shapes and specific weights to minimize visual confusion during reading tasks.\n\nThe source highlights OpenDyslexic as a prominent example of this category of typography, representing an accessibility-focused approach to font design for neurodivergent users benefit.

## Key Takeaways

- Dyslexic fonts are designed to improve readability for people with dyslexia.
- Key features include distinct character shapes and specific weights.
- OpenDyslexic is cited as an example of a dyslexic-friendly font.
