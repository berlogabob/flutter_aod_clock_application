# Burn-in.md

## Summary

Burn-in is a visual degradation phenomenon that occurs primarily on AMOLED and OLED screen technologies. It results in permanent discoloration or "ghosting," where the outlines of previous displays remain visible on the panel.

This effect is caused by displaying static images for extended periods, which leads to uneven wear or chemical changes in the display's organic material components.

## Key Takeaways

- Burn-in results in permanent discoloration or ghosting on displays.
- It is triggered by displaying static images for long periods of time.
- The issue specifically affects AMOLED and OLED screens.
