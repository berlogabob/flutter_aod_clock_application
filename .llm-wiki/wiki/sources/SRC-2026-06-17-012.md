# Skia and CanvasKit.md

## Summary

Skia and CanvasKit serve as the primary graphics engines utilized by the Flutter framework to render high-performance 2D and 3D graphics. Their implementation allows Flutter to deliver consistent visual experiences across a wide range of devices and operating systems.\n\nThe integration of these engines is fundamental to Flutter's ability to handle complex UI elements, animations, and spatial graphics with smooth performance tailored for multiple platforms.

## Key Takeaways

- Skia and CanvasKit are the core graphics engines for Flutter.
- They enable high-performance 2D and 3D graphics rendering.
- These engines provide support for rendering across multiple platforms.
